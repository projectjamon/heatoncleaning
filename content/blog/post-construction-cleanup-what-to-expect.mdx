If you've walked through a space right after the trades finish, you already know: construction dust gets into everything. It's finer than household dust, it settles into vents and tracks, and a standard cleaning pass barely makes a dent.

## Where the dust actually hides

- Window and door tracks
- Vent covers and light fixtures
- Cabinet interiors, even ones that were closed the whole time
- Sticker and label residue on new fixtures and appliances

## Timing matters

The best time to schedule cleanup is after the trades are done but before your final walkthrough — early enough that you're not living with dust, late enough that you're not re-cleaning after a contractor finishes punch-list items.
