Solar panels are marketed as low-maintenance, and mostly they are — but "low-maintenance" doesn't mean "zero-maintenance." Dust, pollen, and bird droppings accumulate gradually enough that most homeowners never notice the buildup by looking at the roof.

## The efficiency cost

Depending on your location and how dusty the season has been, buildup can reduce output by anywhere from a few percent to well over 15% in heavy-dust conditions. On a system sized for a typical home, that's a meaningful chunk of the return you're expecting from the investment.

## Why the cleaning method matters

Not every cleaning approach is safe for solar glass. Abrasive pads or the wrong water can leave mineral spotting or micro-scratches that reduce output further over time — the opposite of the goal. That's why we use deionized water and soft, panel-safe brushes.
