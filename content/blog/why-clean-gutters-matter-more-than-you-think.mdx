It's easy to forget about gutters until water is pouring over the side during a storm. By then, the debris causing the overflow has usually been building for a season or more.

## What actually goes wrong

When gutters clog, water has nowhere to go but over the edge — right next to your foundation, siding, and fascia boards. Over time that means:

- Fascia boards that rot and need replacing
- Landscaping erosion right where water pools
- A higher risk of water finding its way toward your foundation

## The fix is simple

A clearing and downspout flush twice a year — once after spring bloom, once after fall leaf drop — is usually enough to prevent all of it. It's a small, inexpensive habit compared to the repair bill if it's skipped for a few years running.
