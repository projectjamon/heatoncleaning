Most households run on a weekly or biweekly cleaning rhythm — surfaces wiped, floors vacuumed, bathrooms handled. That routine keeps a home looking presentable, but it skips the areas that only show their wear over months: baseboards, grout lines, behind appliances, light fixtures.

## A good starting rule

For most homes, a full deep clean two to four times a year is enough to keep that slower buildup from ever becoming a big job. Homes with pets, young kids, or high foot traffic often do better on the higher end of that range.

## Signs it's time

- Baseboards and trim look dingy even right after a regular clean
- Grout has visibly darkened
- You can't remember the last time behind the fridge was touched
- You're hosting and want the house to feel reset, not just tidied
